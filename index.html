What TechBridge is

TechBridge is a practical training initiative from Baselink Services Limited, built for people who already know the basics and want to prove they can build things that work. Instead of another course to sit through, TechBridge runs short, hands-on programs that end in something you can point to — a project, a portfolio piece, real feedback from real tasks.

It's for students, career-switchers, and self-taught learners in tech who are ready to move past tutorials and into practice.
